Site files

    Add pages, images, styles, scripts, .php files (run in your browser with PHP WebAssembly; visitors get the output, and $_GET / $_POST work) or any other files. Visitors using a peer:// browser (like vav4.3) can open any of them at peer://yourname.peer/filename, and links between your pages work. Your site is saved in this browser, so it comes back after a reload. Add a 404.html to customise the not-found page. The server only runs while this tab stays open.

    Remote editing

    Remote editing is off.
    When on, anyone who knows this password can list, read, create, change, rename and delete your hosted files from Peer Browser (the ✎ button). Pick a strong password and share it only with people you trust. The password is kept in memory only, so it resets when you close this page. Edits replace the in-memory copy that is being hosted; your original files on disk are not touched.
